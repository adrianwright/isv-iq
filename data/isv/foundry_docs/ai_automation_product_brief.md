# AI Automation Product Brief and Qualification Guide

AI Automation is designed for high-volume service workflows that combine intake, classification,
knowledge retrieval, human approval, and downstream system actions. Strong candidates have an
executive sponsor, measurable workflow volume, accessible integration points, and a defined value
baseline.

Before a commercial commitment, the account team must complete an architecture and value workshop.
The workshop confirms priority workflows, data boundaries, integration feasibility, governance,
expected benefits, and required specialist capacity. Product fit does not override unresolved
customer trust or support concerns.
