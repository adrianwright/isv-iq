# Contoso Unified School District Subscription and Premium Support Schedule

Schedule reference: CTR-1001. Customer account: ACC-1001. Renewal record: REN-1001.

This synthetic schedule is a demo artifact. It is not an executed agreement and carries no legal
effect. All parties, amounts, and dates are fictional.

## 1. Parties and purpose

This schedule is between the software provider ("Provider") and Contoso Unified School District
("Customer"), a public school district. It sets out the subscriptions, support entitlements, term,
fees, and renewal mechanics that apply to the Customer's use of the Provider's Core Platform,
Analytics, and Premium Support offerings. It is read together with the Master Subscription Terms
that apply to every Provider customer. Where this schedule and the Master Subscription Terms
disagree, this schedule controls for the matter it addresses.

## 2. Term and renewal

- Start date: December 20, 2024.
- End date: December 20, 2026.
- The agreement does not auto-renew. Continued service after the end date requires a negotiated
  renewal that both parties sign.
- Notice date: October 21, 2026. The Customer is asked to confirm its intent to negotiate a renewal
  on or before this date so that the Provider can reserve implementation and support capacity for
  the next term.
- The requested renewal term is 36 months. A renewal proposal for that term is expected to be
  delivered before the Customer's budget review.

The notice date is a planning milestone, not a termination deadline. Missing it does not extend or
shorten the current term, but it does remove the Provider's obligation to hold the current pricing
structure open.

## 3. Subscriptions and fees

| Subscription | Licensed units | Annual value (USD) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Core Platform | 12,000 | 1,400,000 |
| Analytics | 4,500 | 700,000 |
| Premium Support | 1 (account-level) | 300,000 |

Current annual recurring revenue (ARR) is USD 2,400,000. Fees are invoiced in two installments per
year, on June 30 and September 30. Invoices are payable under the payment terms in the Master
Subscription Terms. Licensed units are named-user entitlements for staff and administrators.

Seat adoption is measured as active units divided by licensed units over a trailing 30 days. The
Provider reports adoption to the Customer at each quarterly business review (QBR). Adoption figures
are informational and do not change fees during the term.

## 4. Premium Support

Premium Support includes:

1. A named support escalation manager for priority-one (P1) incidents.
2. A 24x7 response path for P1 incidents with an initial response target of 30 minutes.
3. A 99.9% monthly uptime service-level objective (SLO) for the production Core Platform and
   Analytics services, excluding scheduled maintenance announced at least five business days in
   advance.
4. Quarterly business reviews and a written incident recovery summary within five business days of
   closing a P1 incident.

### 4.1 Service credits

A substantiated breach of the uptime SLO, or of the P1 response target, may be reviewed for service
credits. Eligibility depends on:

- the incident being recorded in the Provider's incident system with start and end times;
- the Customer having reported the issue through a supported channel;
- the breach not being caused by Customer-managed integrations, Customer network conditions, or
  third-party services outside the Provider's control.

A service credit is a credit against future invoices. It is not a cash refund, and it is the
Customer's sole financial remedy for the affected period. The Provider does not pre-commit to any
credit amount in this schedule. Any credit recommendation must be validated by Support Operations,
Finance, and the account owner before it is presented to the Customer. See the Premium Support
Incident Recovery and Service Credit Policy and the SLA and Service Credit Schedule for the
internal procedure.

### 4.2 Escalation

If a P1 incident remains unresolved for more than five business days, the Provider's support
escalation manager will convene an executive review that includes the account executive and the
Customer's designated technical contact. The Provider will document customer impact, current
mitigation, committed remediation, and items still under engineering investigation.

## 5. Customer responsibilities

The Customer will keep a primary contact and an executive sponsor on file, respond to reasonable
information requests during incident investigation, and complete the identity synchronization
configuration changes recommended in support guidance within a reasonable period.

## 6. Renewal pricing and price protection

A multi-year renewal may include price protection that caps annual uplift for the renewal term.
Price protection is granted only through the approval path in the Strategic Renewal Pricing and
Approval Policy and the Renewal Approval Matrix. Nothing in this schedule commits the Provider to a
specific renewal price.

## 7. Forecasting guidance for the account team

The following statements are internal guidance for interpreting this contract and are not
customer-facing terms:

- Current ARR of USD 2,400,000 is contracted revenue through the end date. It is not a statement of
  renewal confidence.
- A renewal forecast should reflect the probability and value of a signed renewal, not the value of
  the expiring agreement. A forecast below current ARR is appropriate when material renewal risks
  remain open.
- Open P1 incidents with a breached commitment, stalled adoption on a major subscription, or an
  unanswered executive change on the Customer side are each material renewal risks.
- Expansion opportunities are forecast separately from the renewal and must not be added to the
  renewal forecast to offset risk.

## 8. Notices and amendments

Notices are given in writing to the contacts on file. Amendments must be in writing and signed by
both parties. The Provider's sales, support, or success staff cannot amend this schedule orally or
by email.

## 9. Order of precedence and general terms

Order of precedence: (1) signed amendments, (2) this schedule, (3) the Master Subscription Terms,
(4) Provider policies referenced by name. General terms on confidentiality, data protection,
limitation of liability, and governing law are in the Master Subscription Terms.
