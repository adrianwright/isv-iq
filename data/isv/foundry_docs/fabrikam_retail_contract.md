## 1. Parties and purpose

This schedule is between the software provider ("Provider") and Fabrikam Unified School District
("Customer"), a public school district. It sets out the subscriptions, support entitlements, term,
fees, and renewal and expansion mechanics for the Customer's use of Core Platform, Analytics, and
Premium Support. It is read together with the Master Subscription Terms.

## 4. Premium Support

Premium Support includes a named support escalation manager, a 24x7 response path for priority-one
(P1) incidents, quarterly business reviews, and a 99.95% monthly uptime service-level objective
(SLO) for production Core Platform and Analytics services. Scheduled maintenance announced at least
five business days in advance is excluded.

Service credits for a substantiated SLO breach are reviewed under the same procedure that applies
to every Provider customer. The Customer has no open P1 cases. The most recent support item, a
school identity synchronization issue, was resolved within the service-level commitment.

## 5. Expansion amendment

The agreement supports an expansion amendment for the AI Automation product when commercial and
architecture criteria are satisfied. The criteria are:

1. An executive sponsor on the Customer side confirms commercial sponsorship.
2. A completed architecture review validates security, integration, capacity, and measurable value
   criteria.
3. The Provider's account owner, Finance, and the designated executive sponsor approve the
   proposal under the Strategic Renewal Pricing and Approval Policy.
4. Provider specialist capacity is confirmed for implementation.

The latest architecture review approved the proposed AI Automation rollout, and the Customer's
executive sponsor confirmed commercial sponsorship. The estimated expansion value is USD 300,000
in annual recurring revenue. Expansion revenue is forecast separately from the renewal.
