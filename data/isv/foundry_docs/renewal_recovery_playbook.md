# At-Risk Renewal Recovery Playbook

An at-risk strategic renewal should have one integrated recovery plan covering service,
adoption, commercial, and relationship signals. The first customer package should include the
incident recovery summary, committed support follow-ups, the adoption recovery milestone, and the
renewal proposal timeline.

Open commitments require named owners and dates. Expansion discovery may continue in parallel,
but a new product proposal should be positioned as a validated customer outcome rather than a
substitute for remediation. Executive outreach, pricing, service credits, roadmap statements, and
commercial commitments require human review before release.
