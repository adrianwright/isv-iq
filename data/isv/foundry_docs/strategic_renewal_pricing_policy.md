# Strategic Renewal Pricing and Approval Policy

Strategic customers may receive a multi-year proposal with price protection when the account team
documents customer value, renewal risk, payment status, support exposure, and executive
sponsorship. A three-year term requires approval from the account owner, Finance, and the
designated executive sponsor.

An overdue invoice does not automatically block a proposal, but payment timing must be confirmed
before final approval. Pricing must not be used to conceal unresolved service risk. When recovery
work remains open, the proposal should state the dependency and preserve a human approval gate.
