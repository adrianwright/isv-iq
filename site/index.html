Reconciliation The finding Method Limits Source

One sales renewal question, answered from sources nobody keeps together.

A software company has to decide whether to raise a renewal forecast. The evidence sits with finance, legal, the account team and the outside world. This demo reads across all of them, shows its sources, and stops at a draft for a person to review.

Guided reconciliation

The system decides, grounded in your rules.

Evidence from every source lands in one place, and the system settles the answer. It does not leave that to whoever read the last email. Each source is trusted for what it knows best, and your rules set the order: a contract for terms, the finance system for money, the account team for promises, the web for the outside world. Claims are weighed by kind, never averaged.

Records win

An open priority-one case, a falling adoption trend or an overdue invoice caps the verdict at "watch", however upbeat the narrative around it. The conflict is written down, not smoothed over.

The worst signal holds

Evidence can lower a status but never raise it. Confidence falls with each conflict and each source that could not be reached, so a thin answer says so.

The model explains, it does not decide

A model writes the briefing from facts that are already settled. If it changes the verdict or cites evidence that does not exist, its text is discarded and the plain draft is used.

“Should we increase the Contoso Unified School District renewal forecast from $2.2M to the full $2.4M?”

The question, as asked

The finding

Not yet. Hold the forecast at $2.2M.

Verdict

Not yet

Two open priority-one cases and a missing executive response stand between this renewal and the full amount. A drafted next action names an owner. It has not been submitted.

  • The usage and billing data support the larger figure, but two P1 cases are still open against the contract’s service commitments.
  • The account team promised the customer a response that the record does not show as delivered.
  • Approval rules in the contract require resolution before the forecast moves.

1Fabric IQ: cases, SLA breaches and seat adoption.

2Work IQ: huddle notes and customer correspondence.

3Foundry IQ: contract and approval policy.

Method

Parallel retrieval, stated uncertainty, a person at the end.

  1. Interpret the questionResolve the account, the renewal, the decision asked for and what is missing.
  2. Retrieve in parallelAsk every connected source at the same time, and time each one.
  3. Guided reconciliationSeparate what is met from blockers, contradictions and gaps.
  4. Draft the next actionReturn citations, an owner and a task that stays unsubmitted.

Limits

What this is, and what it is not.

Connected

Live Foundry, Fabric, Work and Web integrations run on Azure. Fabric holds the structured data and Microsoft 365 holds the team context.

Reproducible

Built to be deployed to Azure. Where live services are unavailable, the walkthrough falls back to deterministic synthetic data so a demo always behaves the same.

Fictional

Every district, contract, person and figure is invented. The output is a draft, not a commitment or a forecast of record.
